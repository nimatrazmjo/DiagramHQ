## Summary

- **Encryption at Rest & in Transit**: Authenticated AES-256-GCM encryption at rest, Customer-Managed Encryption Keys (CMEK) via AWS KMS/GCP KMS/Vault, envelope encryption, automated key rotation (<= 90 days), TLS 1.3 requirement, 1-year HSTS preload, and Perfect Forward Secrecy (PFS).

- **Enterprise Security Review Checklist**: SOC2, ISO 27001, and NIST SP 800-53 aligned checklist covering 6 domains (`network_transport`, `cryptography_rest`, `identity_access`, `audit_logging`, `resilience_dr`, `vulnerability_mgmt`).
- **Web UI & Drill Simulator**: Interactive `EnterpriseSecurityModal` with executive KPI ribbon, category/status filters, real-time checklist evaluation, live DR drill simulator, and crypto-shredding tester.
