# Pull Request: F132 — Billing, Plans & Usage Quota Management

## Summary
Implements monetization tiers, enterprise feature gating, and usage quota enforcement across the DiagramHQ platform:
- **Tier Definitions**: Free, Pro ($20-24/seat/mo), Business ($49-59/seat/mo), and Enterprise ($120-149/seat/mo).
- **Entitlements & Feature Gating**: Fine-grained feature mapping (`FEATURE_MINIMUM_TIERS`) covering AI Copilot, GitLab/GitHub sync, clean PDF/SVG export, SSO/SAML, SCIM provisioning, organization policies, advanced RBAC, compliance packs, private VPC deployment, and custom SLA support.
- **Enforcement Mechanics**:
  - `enforceTierGating`: Throws `TierGatingError` when an organization on a lower tier attempts to access higher-tier features.
  - `enforceUsageLimit`: Throws `UsageLimitExceededError` when an operation exceeds quantitative tier quotas (seats, architecture objects, views, monthly AI query credits, audit log retention window).
- **Subscription Lifecycle & Invoices**: Seamless subscription upgrades, annual/monthly billing cycles with automated 17-20% discounts, and invoice calculation (`calculateInvoice`).
- **Web UI & Governance Ribbon**: Accessible `BillingModal` with multi-tier comparison cards, live usage consumption progress meters with warning thresholds, full capability matrix, interactive enforcement simulator, and upgrade flows.
