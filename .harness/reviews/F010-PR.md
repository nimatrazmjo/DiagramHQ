# PR: F010 — Pan and Zoom

## Summary
- Implements infinite pan and zoom with wheel zoom limits (`0.1x` to `4.0x`) and clamping helpers.
- Adds space-pan drag activation (`panActivationKeyCode="Space"`, Space key event listeners, grab cursors, and visual badge).
- Adds fit-to-content shortcut ('F' key) and top-right toolbar with Zoom In (+), Zoom Out (−), Reset (100%), and Fit (F).
- Enhances `useCanvasStore` with transient zoom helpers (`zoomIn`, `zoomOut`, `resetZoom`, `clampZoom`) strictly isolated from domain models (Layer Boundaries Rule 4).
- Adds 10 automated unit and component tests in `apps/web/pan-zoom.spec.ts` for a monorepo total of 235 passing tests.
