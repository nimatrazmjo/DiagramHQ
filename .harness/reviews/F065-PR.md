# PR: F065 — Architecture explanation

## Feature Summary
Implemented pure AI Architecture Explanation engine and audience-calibrated narrative drawer for DiagramHQ (Phase 08 — AI Copilot). Generates grounded textual, structural, and operational explanations of systems, flows, components, and architectural decision records (ADRs) tailored across three distinct altitudes:
- Technical / Engineer: In-depth protocols (HTTP/gRPC/Kafka), sync vs. async RPC semantics, ports, error boundaries, and payload transit paths
- Architectural / Solutions Architect: Bounded contexts, afferent/efferent coupling degrees, CAP theorem trade-offs, scalability bottlenecks, and integration topologies
- Executive / CTO: Business capabilities, operational continuity, blast radius, risk posture, and team alignment
Strict invariant: Every explanation is fully grounded in the live model graph and cites concrete `ObjectId` and `ConnectionId` instances. Added canvas UI components `<AltitudeSelector />` and `<ArchitectureExplanationPanel />` with multi-altitude switcher, summary callout, section breakdown with bulleted key points, and interactive grounded entity citations.
